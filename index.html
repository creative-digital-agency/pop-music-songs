About this music library

What is Pop Music Songs?

Pop Music Songs is an interactive album and track library where you can discover album descriptions, upload audio, and listen to your own collection.

How do I add a music album?

Select “Add music,” enter an album title, artist, genre, and description, and save. You can add more songs to any album later.

Which audio formats can I upload?

Common browser-supported formats include MP3, WAV, OGG and M4A. FLAC support depends on your browser. Uploaded audio is stored in this browser on your device, not published online.
